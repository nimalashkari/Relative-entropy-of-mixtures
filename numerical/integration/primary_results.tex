\paragraph{Numerical check of primary relative entropies.}
The same lattice primary states used below for mixtures also give
an independent check of the pure-state leading terms, by directly
evaluating $S(\rho_a^A\|\rho_b^A)$.
Table~\ref{tab:num-primary} summarizes a free fit $Cx^{\widehat\alpha}$
on 51 geometries per direction, with $\ell=6,\ldots,10$,
$N\leq256$, and $x\leq0.08$. Both parameters are fitted, using
the spectral evaluation and least-squares procedure of
Appendix~\ref{app:num-methods}. These checks use a separate,
smaller-chain data set from the mixture results in
Sec.~\ref{sec:num-examples}.

The boson exponents lie between $2.0050$ and $2.0090$, and its
amplitudes differ from the leading predictions by at most $2.41\%$.
For Ising, $I\leftrightarrow\sigma$ gives exponents near $2.004$,
whereas $I\leftrightarrow\varepsilon$ gives $3.8376,3.9196$.
The spin--energy directions have appreciable finite-window
corrections: their exponents are $2.2160,2.3071$, and their fitted
amplitudes are $1.7566,2.4036$, compared with $\pi^2/12$.
Thus this independent check is compatible with several leading
predictions but does not establish uniform asymptotic agreement
over this historical window. In particular, fitting a good curve
does not by itself validate both asymptotic parameters.
